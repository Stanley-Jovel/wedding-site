## Travel

**ACCOMMODATION:**
We recommend booking an AirBnb or Vrbo within the city of San Salvador. Accommodation in El Salvador is much cheaper than the U.S., but can be pricey in more gentrified areas. Booking a larger apartment or house and sharing with others cuts down costs significantly.

If you are interested in being put in touch with other wedding guests to share a booking, please contact Annika.

**TRANSPORTATION:**
The wedding will take place in Zaragoza, about a 20-30 minute drive away from San Salvador. Driving is the most feasible form of transportation, and you can drive with a U.S. driver's license in El Salvador.

If you will have an extended stay in the country, we recommend renting a car. Otherwise, Uber is a convenient and affordable option.

**CURRENCY:**
El Salvador's national currency is the U.S. dollar. If you plan to do any shopping, we recommend bringing cash for street & informal vendors. Tipping is not customary.

**Is there a dress code?**
Please join us in semi-formal attire.

That means knee-length or midi-length dresses, jumpsuits, or pantsuits in an elevated fabric; a button-up shirt and dress pants (tie and blazer optional); elevated shoes such as loafers, oxfords, leather dress sneakers, heels, or ballet flats. The ceremony and reception will be held on a grass lawn, so block heels or flats are recommended.

In March in El Salvador, the temperature ranges from 70-90 degrees Fahrenheit (21-32 °C) with moderate to high humidity. There is almost no chance of rain.

The bridal party's colors are white and green. Please keep this in mind when selecting an outfit to ensure the bridal party stands out.

No jeans.
